NPM-N thresholds used

Nutrient / criterionThreshold
Saturated fat≥10% of total energy
Trans fat≥1% of total energy
Free sugarsSolid foods: ≥10% of total energy; beverages: ≥5% of total energy
SodiumSolid foods: ≥1 mg/kcal OR ≥300 mg/100 g; caloric beverages: ≥1 mg/kcal OR ≥300 mg/100 mL; non-caloric beverages: ≥40 mg/100 mL

The calculator accepts nutrient amounts per serving and automatically standardizes sodium to a per-100 g or per-100 mL basis where required. Energy-based nutrient thresholds are calculated from the nutrient and energy amounts reported for the same serving.
